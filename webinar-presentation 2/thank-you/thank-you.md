# Thank You — Closing Slide

---

## Slide 1 — Thank you / CTA

Single hero slide. The QR is the slide. One focal point, hard to ignore, designed to maximise scans.

### Layout

- **Top-right corner — pinned ribbon (small, secondary):**
  *Drop your questions in the Q&A — we'll get to them.*

- **Above the QR — hairline header (small, intimate):**
  *Thank you*

- **Centre — hero element:**
  Large QR code, treated like a portal. Soft glow / nebula bloom behind it. Thin rounded border. The QR is the largest object on the slide by a wide margin.

- **Below the QR — the carrot (single line, the actual headline):**
  **Take it home.**

- **Under the carrot — gate hint (folds deliverables + gate into one line):**
  *Two quick questions → slides, code & data.*
  One line carries both the pre-disclosure of the gate and what they get. Matches the questionnaire's own copy.

- **Bottom — fallback URL (small, mono):**
  In case the QR doesn't scan, a short readable URL underneath.

### Copy — final text

> *Thank you*
>
> **Take it home.**
>
> *Two quick questions → slides, code & data.*
>
> *Drop your questions in the Q&A — we'll get to them.*

### Visual notes

- Cosmic baseline: deep-space background, drifting nebulae, starfield specks — same language as the rest of the deck.
- QR sits on a subtle white/cream tile so it scans cleanly against the dark background. Tile has a soft glow (blue → purple → orange, the Cosmic palette) bleeding outward.
- Hierarchy: QR > carrot line > thank you > unlock list > Q&A ribbon. The eye lands on the QR first; the carrot tells them *why* to scan; everything else is supporting.
- One slide, no animation beyond the ambient nebula drift and a gentle pulse on the QR glow.

### CTA destination

QR + fallback URL both point to the same landing page: a short questionnaire (lead gen) gated in front of the content bundle (presentations, code, data, links). The questionnaire is the ask; the content is the reward.
