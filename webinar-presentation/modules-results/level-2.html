What worked · what didn’t

Level 2 — reading the cohort.

Worked

Where the cohort delivered.

  • Best yaw: A10 at 51.0% off V0 — every agent beat the no-op baseline.
  • Best CTE: A02 at 59.4% — the two KPIs move together when the model is honest about lag.
  • Median yaw improvement 49.8%, median CTE 57.0% — the cohort clusters, not scatters.
vs
Didn’t

Where it left room.

  • Yaw spread 4.9 pp across the cohort — the bottom agents leave performance on the table.
  • Weakest yaw: A08 at 46.1%, weakest CTE: A10 at 54.7%.
  • Per-platform RMSE always worst on Ford trucks — high mass, high lag, the model can’t hide it.
