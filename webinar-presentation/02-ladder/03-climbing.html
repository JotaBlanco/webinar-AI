The Ladder

Climb.

Five rungs from a chatbot to a harness. The model stays the same at every rung — what grows up is the system around it.

L4AI Harness

The workplace itself.

Governance, scheduling, sub-agents, audit. AI as infrastructure, not a tool.

L3+ Knowledge Base

Institutional memory.

Domain facts, past decisions, customer context — like a veteran teammate.

L2+ Skills

Muscle memory.

Reusable team procedures — acts like someone who's done this before.

L1Agentic AI

Hands.

Reads your files, runs commands, opens PRs. Acts inside your environment.

L0Chatbot

Conversation.

Answers questions in a vacuum. Generic knowledge, no tools, no context — copy-paste is the integration.

At the top · L4

A bare agent is a poltergeist.
The harness makes it a lamp.

L1 is a model with hands and no rails — power without containment. L4 is the same model, harnessed: scoped, observed, governed, and safe enough to operate as infrastructure instead of a tool.
