Version 2 · Staircase

Five rungs, one direction: outward.

From a developer's pocket tool to the company's operating system.

L0 · Chatbot
Personal productivity
One user. One window. One question at a time. The AI is a smart pair of eyes you walk over to.
L1 · Agentic AI
Personal autopilot
One developer, now with hands. The AI reads files, runs commands, opens PRs — but still works in one head.
L2 · + Skills
Team conventions
Reusable procedures capture how the team actually works. Every agent run inherits team practice, not one person's prompts.
L3 · + Knowledge Base
Team intelligence
Shared context the whole team contributes to and draws from. The blank-slate problem disappears.
L4 · AI Harness
Org infrastructure
Governed, scheduled, multi-agent, audited. AI runs in CI, on rotations, across services — woven into how the company operates.
